## Notes
- Analysis facts (verified in worktree at 36d3e5b):
  - `R/stream_connection.R:17` reads "...covers at least 16 MiB, so" (sentence spans lines 16-18).
  - `R/arrow.R:23-24` reads "bytes are fetched on demand in windows of at least" / "#' 16 MiB, aligned to 64 KiB."
  - `src/rust/src/lib.rs:23-24` sets `READ_WINDOW_MIN` = 1 MiB and `READ_WINDOW_MAX` = 16 MiB. `lib.rs:176` sets the initial window to `(size / 32).clamp(MIN, MAX)`. `lib.rs:106` doubles it while reads move forward, capped at MAX. So "at least 16 MiB" is wrong; the floor is 1 MiB.
  - `README.Rmd:99` and `AGENTS.md:189` already state "at least 1 MiB (larger while reads move forward, up to 16 MiB)". Matching that wording.
- Proposed wording, `R/stream_connection.R` (lines 16-18 become four lines, same paragraph, same content otherwise):
  ```
  #' Random access works by asking Xet for a byte range. Each miss fetches a
  #' window that starts on a 64 KiB boundary and covers at least 1 MiB, growing
  #' toward 16 MiB while reads move forward, so sequential and column-oriented
  #' reads are served mostly from the window.
  ```
- Proposed wording, `R/arrow.R` (lines 23-24):
  ```
  #' **Partial download:** bytes are fetched on demand in windows of at least
  #' 1 MiB (larger while reads move forward, up to 16 MiB), aligned to 64 KiB.
  ```
