## Notes
- **Verified skip mechanism (probed in scratch, not the package):** with testthat 3.3.2, `setup` is `eval_tidy(enquo(code))` evaluated immediately at file-source time; a `skip_if_not_installed()` raised inside `setup({...})` skips the ENTIRE file cleanly (probe with a bogus package reported both tests skipped, 0 failures). This is why `test-concurrent.R` and `test-stream-range.R` need the guard inside `setup()`, not per-test: `create_test_fixtures()` (called from `setup()` at lines 6 of both files) invokes `uuid::UUIDgenerate()` unconditionally at `helper-mock-backend.R:78,86,100`.
- **`uuid` call sites:** `helper-mock-backend.R:78,86,100`; `test-concurrent.R` (setup + lines 18,35,44,86,167,204); `test-edge-cases.R:114`. Already-covered after the two setup guards + one per-test guard.
- **Already gated (no change):** `test-parallel-downloads.R:61` `skip_if_not_installed("future.apply")`; `test-arrow-spatial.R:5,16` `skip_if_not(requireNamespace("arrow", ...))`; `test-arrow-spatial.R:28–47` terra/sf tests gate themselves.
- **No guard for `future`:** referenced nowhere in tests (only `future.apply`).
- **Environment:** R 4.6.1, testthat 3.3.2, devtools 2.5.2. Missing `uuid`, `arrow`, `covr`; present `future.apply`, `sf`, `terra`.
- `devtools::test()` defaults to `stop_on_failure = FALSE`; the flag is mandatory for acceptance.
